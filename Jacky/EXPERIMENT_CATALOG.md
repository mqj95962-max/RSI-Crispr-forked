# Experiment catalog (whole project)

One table of load-bearing experiments across the repo — bacterial SLICER work,
mechanism / CRISPRi controls, head-to-head with crisprHAL, transfer / human
boundary, and orientation. No attribution by person.

**How to read the columns**

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| **Experiment** | Short name of the analysis |
| **Finding** | What it showed (key numbers) |
| **Code** | Committed runner; evidence CSV/JSON under `Runjia/results/` unless noted. If the producer is missing, that is stated. |
| **Project impact** | How it moved the overall story |

**Frames:** **[P]** = Noshay published 13,880-guide matrix; **[C]** = crisprHAL curated ~33.5k. They are not interchangeable.

**Paths:** `Runjia/sgrna/...` and `Runjia/colab/...` are the bacterial pipeline; `Jacky/...` holds orientation and human-boundary runners. Evidence files are relative to `Runjia/results/` unless a full path is given.
